# Take the ClearPath!

Clear is a member-owned cooperative building financial infrastructure that connects savings, credit, and ownership — on rails we operate ourselves.

We're designing this to be useful for everyday people, local businesses, and developers, not just crypto-native teams.

## Mission

**Turning renters into owners.**
**Stop Renting. Start Owning. Take the Clear path.**

---

## Where we're starting

Before anyone owns a home, they need a way to build credit and savings that doesn't work against them. So the first thing we're putting in front of real people is **credit at the counter of local businesses**.

A customer finances a repair, a treatment, or a purchase over a few cycles — **no credit check** — and the shop is paid on ordinary commercial terms. The customer's repayments build savings and equity credits that count toward a home. The shop pays less than card processing and bears no default risk.

**What that looks like in practice:**

- **For members** — savings-backed credit that costs nothing, term plans for larger purchases, and a card that spends cash before it spends credit
- **For merchants** — a browser-based counter app, net-30 payouts, no setup fee, no hardware, no exclusivity
- **For both** — partner membership in the cooperative, one member one vote

We're signing our first **founding merchant partners** in the Inland Empire now. If you run a business where customers regularly face a bill they'd rather spread out — auto repair, dental, veterinary, trades, appliances — [get in touch](mailto:hello@useclear.org).

## The platform, in two layers

### 1) Financial rails

The primitives that make the above work, built to be reused rather than rebuilt per product.

- **Clear Savings (ESA)** — equity-oriented savings that back a member's own credit line
- **Clear Credit** — a waterfall that spends the cheapest source first: savings-backed at no cost, then asset-backed, then income-backed
- **Clear Cash™ and partner credit** — fixed-term plans, priced per cycle on the declining balance
- **Clear USD (`$CLRUSD`)** — programmable settlement between members, merchants, and the co-op
- **Clear Assurance** — reserve and loss-absorption mechanics sized against real exposure, not total credit outstanding

### 2) Ownership and RWA rails

Real-world assets on-chain, with legal-grade ownership records and programmable behaviour.

- Deeds and titles represented through protocol contracts
- Asset lifecycle tracking from issuance through transfer
- Programmable ownership logic for resale, permissions, and value-sharing
- **Clear Equity (ELPA)** — the ownership path itself, unlocked by equity credits earned through saving

## On the roadmap

Real intentions, not current capability. We would rather say so than imply otherwise.

- **Clear Tag Network** — RFID and microchip tags embedded in physical goods, for authenticity, provenance, resale control, and loyalty. Designed; not yet built. It comes after the merchant corridor is standing on its own.
- **A home chain** — a unified environment for financial, ownership, and item-identity rails. Further out, and not being worked on today.

## Who Clear is for

- **Members** — build savings and credit at the same time, and reach ownership through both
- **Local businesses** — offer financing without underwriting risk, and join the co-op that runs it
- **Developers** — build against open, composable protocol components
- **Cooperators** — one member, one vote, including the businesses in the network
